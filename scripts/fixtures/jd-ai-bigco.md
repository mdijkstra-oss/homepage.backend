# Software Engineer III, Full Stack — Assisted Claims Experiences (Meridian Mutual)

**Org:** Claims Technology · **Team:** Assisted Claims Experiences
**Location:** Columbus, OH or Hartford, CT · Hybrid, 3 days in office (Tue/Wed/Thu)
**Level:** SWE III (IC3) · **Requisition:** R-48219

## About the company and the team

Meridian Mutual is a publicly traded property and casualty insurer with more than 6,400 employees across North America. Claims Technology is one of six product groups in our technology organization, and the Assisted Claims Experiences team sits inside it. We own the AI-assisted surfaces our claims adjusters use every day: document summarization for first notice of loss, a retrieval-backed policy lookup assistant, and drafting help for adjuster correspondence. The platform already exists and serves roughly 9,000 internal users. You will be joining an established codebase and roadmap, taking ownership of a slice of it — not standing up something new from zero.

## What you will do

- Build and maintain features across our React and TypeScript adjuster console and the Java (Spring Boot) services behind it, plus Python services for prompt orchestration and evaluation.
- Take work from quarterly planning through design, implementation, rollout behind LaunchDarkly flags, and post-release monitoring in Datadog.
- Partner day to day with a product manager, a designer, and a data scientist on your pod; review model-facing changes with Legal and our AI Governance Council before launch.
- Extend our evaluation harness and add regression cases when adjusters report bad summaries or citations.
- Work with the Model Platform team, who own inference infrastructure and the model gateway, to integrate new capabilities into your surfaces.
- Participate in a shared on-call rotation (one week in six, business hours primary) and in design reviews across Claims Technology.

## Minimum qualifications

- 3+ years of professional software engineering experience building and operating web applications.
- Proficiency in TypeScript and a modern front-end framework, plus a server-side language such as Java, Python, Go, or C#.
- Experience with relational databases and REST or gRPC service design.
- Experience shipping to production in a team using code review, CI/CD, and automated testing.
- Bachelor's degree in a technical field or equivalent practical experience.

## Preferred qualifications

- Experience integrating LLM APIs or retrieval systems into a production product, including evaluation and prompt regression testing.
- Familiarity with AWS (EKS, RDS, S3), Kafka, or Terraform.
- Experience working in a regulated industry with audit, privacy, or compliance review in the release path.
