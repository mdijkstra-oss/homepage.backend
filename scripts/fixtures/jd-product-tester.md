# Product Tester — Halden Audio (Utrecht)

Halden Audio builds the Corran range of hearing-assist earbuds and the Halden companion app for iOS and Android. The app handles fitting, hearing profiles, firmware updates and the live sound controls people use in a noisy restaurant. A tester here works with real hardware: pairing and re-pairing units, walking through fittings, pulling firmware onto pre-production devices and confirming the app behaves the same on a five year old Android phone as it does on the newest iPhone. Much of what matters cannot be caught by an automated suite, which is why this role exists.

## What you will do

- Execute test scripts from our test case management system against nightly and release candidate builds, and record a clear pass or fail for every step
- Run regression passes across our supported device matrix ahead of each release — this is repetitive by design, and doing it carefully is the point
- Write defect reports that a developer can act on without asking you a follow-up question: steps, device, build number, expected, actual, logs, video where it helps
- Verify fixes on the build they landed in, and reopen when the fix does not hold
- Check features against the acceptance criteria on the ticket and flag where the build and the criteria disagree
- Do exploratory sessions on new features, then turn what you find into permanent test cases
- Sit in on refinement and release calls and say plainly whether a build is ready

## What we are looking for

- Genuine attention to detail, and the patience to run the same script for the ninth time as carefully as the first
- Clear written English — most of your output is text other people rely on
- Familiarity with an issue tracker (Jira, Linear or similar) and some form of test case management (TestRail, Xray, Zephyr, or a well kept spreadsheet)
- Comfort working inside a defined process, and the confidence to push back when a build is not good enough
- Careful handling of pre-production hardware

No degree required. No coding required — writing code is not part of this job, and we are not hiring an SDET.

## Nice to have

- Experience testing a phone app paired with a physical device over Bluetooth
- Any exposure to accessibility testing or to hearing, audio or medical products
- Dutch, German or French alongside English, for localisation checks

## The arrangement

Permanent, full time, 40 hours. Three days a week in our Utrecht office — the hardware lives here. This is a junior to medior individual contributor role reporting to our QA Lead; it is not a management track. €2,900 – €3,600 gross per month depending on experience, plus 8% holiday allowance and 25 days leave.
